Privacy

Privacy Policy

RoadBloom (the “App”) is an iOS app that records drives and visualizes the HEX cells you travel through. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

Effective and last updated: October 1, 2026

01Information processed and stored

Location

When you start a drive, the App records precise location, timestamp, accuracy, speed, and direction. If you enable background recording, an active drive continues while the App is in the background or the screen is locked.

Vehicle details and photo

The App stores the vehicle name, odometer value, and vehicle photo you optionally provide. Background removal and resizing of the photo are performed on your device.

App data

The App stores drive history, routes, unlocked HEX cells, statistics, Replay access, preferences, and data used by its widgets.

Advertising data

Through Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform, Google and advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device information, advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, approximate location, and consent choices.

02Purposes

  • Provide distance, route, visited HEX cells, history, statistics, and Replay
  • Continue an active drive in the background or while the screen is locked
  • Display your vehicle and provide widgets
  • Deliver banner ads and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch
  • Manage advertising consent, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and diagnose failures

03On-device data and external transfers

RoadBloom does not upload your precise drive routes or vehicle photo to the operator's servers.

This information remains on your device. The App has no account registration. RoadBloom also does not send precise drive routes to the advertising SDK.

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent management. The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is used only if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you can still use the App's drive-recording features.

Processing by Google and other advertising providers varies by your region, choices, device settings, and the ads delivered. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising

04Retention and deletion

On-device data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App. You can delete individual drives in the App. From Settings, you can also delete all saved and unfinished drives, routes, unlocked HEX cells, Replay access, and drive-derived totals. Vehicle details and a manually entered odometer value can be removed through their editing controls or by deleting the App.

Information processed by Google or other external providers is retained and deleted according to each provider's policies.

05Your controls

  • You can change location and tracking permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Where applicable, you can change advertising consent through RoadBloom Settings → Advertising privacy options.
  • If you decline tracking, you can still use the App's core non-advertising features, including drive recording.
  • If you decline location access, drive recording is unavailable.

06Security and children's privacy

Information stored on your device is kept in the app-specific storage provided by iOS. The App is not designed primarily for children and does not intend to knowingly collect personal information from children.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App, external services, or applicable rules change. Material changes will be announced on this page or in the App. The revised policy applies from the date it is posted here.
